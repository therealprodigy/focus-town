# Asset credits

## Fonts

Silkscreen Regular and Bold by Jason Kottke and VT323 Regular by Peter Hull are bundled from the Google Fonts repository under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their full copyright and license notices are in public/fonts/Silkscreen-OFL.txt and public/fonts/VT323-OFL.txt.

Sources:
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/silkscreen
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/vt323

## Artwork

The characters, terrain, buildings and lighting are original Canvas pixel drawings. No Terraria or Stardew Valley sprites are bundled. Stardew Valley official screenshots informed the terrain detail; Terraria was the owner's requested art reference.
